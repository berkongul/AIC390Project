Leading an area means owning its quality, not working alone on it. Everyone writes code, and each member has commits in at least two of the three areas by week 7 (rule R2).

## How we work

- **Channels:** the team WhatsApp group for quick questions, GitHub for all work and evidence.
- **Meeting** *(proposed)*: one 30-minute online meeting every week on [day] at [time], to read the scoreboard and split the next week's deliverables by ID.
- **Internal deadline** *(proposed)*: all work for the week is merged by Monday 20:00. The report is finished and checked on Tuesday morning, and the link goes on the course page at least 24 hours before class (rule R4).
- **Presenter:** rotates every week so each member presents at least twice. Order *(proposed)*: Selim (week 1), Berk (week 2), Orhun (week 3), then repeat.
- **Code:** work on a branch, open a pull request, and another member reviews it before merge. No API keys in the repository (R13). Every number in a report comes from a script in the repository (R8).

## How we decide *(proposed)*

Discuss in the group, then decide by majority vote. Inside their own area, the lead makes the technical call unless the other two both disagree.

## When someone doesn't deliver *(proposed)*

Tell the group as soon as you know, and at least 24 hours before the internal deadline. The other two can take the task over, and the contributions table records who did the work. If it happens twice, we raise it in the weekly report or with the instructor, as handbook section 8 asks, instead of waiting until week 7.

## Ownership after the course *(proposed)*

The three members own the project equally, subject to the university's intellectual property policy (R20). Each of us may show it in a portfolio. Turning it into a product or company needs a new written agreement signed by all three.
