# Size guide: shoes

## Shoe sizes
Our shoes come in EU sizes. Measure your foot from heel to the tip of the longest toe and use this table. EU 40 fits 25.5 cm, EU 41 fits 26.0 cm, EU 42 fits 26.7 cm, EU 43 fits 27.3 cm, EU 44 fits 28.0 cm and EU 45 fits 28.7 cm.

## Fit by model
Running and trail shoes, including the TR-220, fit about half a size small. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size. Hiking boots and canvas sneakers fit true to size.

## Half sizes
We only sell whole EU sizes.
