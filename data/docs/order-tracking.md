# Order tracking

## Tracking code
When your order is dispatched, we email you a tracking code. The code also appears on the Orders page of your account.

## When tracking starts
The carrier's first scan can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch, so a new tracking code may show no events at first.

## Order statuses
Preparing means the order has not left our warehouse yet. Dispatched means the carrier has the parcel. Delivered means the carrier recorded the delivery.
