# Changing or cancelling an order

## Before dispatch
While the order status is Preparing, you can cancel the order or change the delivery address from the Orders page or in chat. Cancelled orders are refunded within 5 business days.

## After dispatch
Once the order is Dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled or redirected. You can refuse the delivery or return the items under the returns policy.

## Adding items
We cannot add items to an order that was already placed. Please place a new order.
