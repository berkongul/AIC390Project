# Size guide: trousers

## Waist and inside leg
Trousers are sized by waist in inches. Waist 30 fits 76 to 79 cm, 32 fits 81 to 84 cm, 34 fits 86 to 89 cm and 36 fits 91 to 94 cm. Every waist size comes in inside leg lengths of 30, 32 and 34 inches.

## Fit
Our chinos have a regular fit through the hip and a slightly tapered leg.
