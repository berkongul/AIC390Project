# Care instructions

## Cotton clothing
Wash cotton t-shirts, shirts and hoodies at 30 degrees, inside out, with similar colours. Do not tumble dry hoodies, to keep their shape.

## Merino wool
Wash merino socks and beanies at 30 degrees on a wool cycle and dry them flat. Do not tumble dry merino.

## Rain shell jacket
Close all zippers before washing. Wash at 30 degrees without fabric softener. Re-apply a water-repellent spray when water stops beading on the surface.

## Shoes
Clean shoes with a soft brush and cold water. Do not put shoes in the washing machine, because this voids the warranty.
