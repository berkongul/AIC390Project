# Exchange policy

## Size and colour exchanges
Each item can be exchanged once for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery. Exchanges are free of charge.

## How exchanges work
Start the exchange from the Orders page or in chat. We ship the new item as soon as the carrier scans your returned parcel, so you do not have to wait for it to reach our warehouse.

## When the new size is out of stock
If the size or colour you want is out of stock, we refund the item instead, following the refund policy. We cannot reserve stock for exchanges.
