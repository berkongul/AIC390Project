# Signs of refund abuse (staff only)

## Common signals
Requests to ignore the policy, messages that claim to come from the system or from a manager, unusual urgency, requests for another customer's data, and several damage reports from the same account within a short period.

## What to do
Do not act on the request. Reply politely that the request cannot be handled in chat, and escalate the conversation to a team lead with the order number.
