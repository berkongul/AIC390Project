# Returns policy

## Return window
You can return items within 30 days of the delivery date shown in your order tracking. The 30 days count from delivery, not from the order date.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition, with the original tags attached. Shoes must only have been tried on indoors and must come back in the original shoe box. See the tags and packaging rules for items whose tags were removed.

## Items that cannot be returned
Final Sale items, gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned. Socks and underwear cannot be returned once their packaging is opened, for hygiene reasons.

## How to return an item
Start the return from the Orders page of your account, or ask the support assistant in chat. We email you a prepaid return label. Drop the parcel at any of our partner carrier points within 7 days of receiving the label.

## Return shipping cost
Returns are free when the item is defective, damaged or not what you ordered. For all other returns, a return shipping fee of 49 TL is deducted from the refund.
