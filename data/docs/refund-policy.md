# Refund policy

## When the refund is issued
We inspect returned items within 2 business days of their arrival at our warehouse. Once an item passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## When the money appears
After we issue the refund, your bank may take up to 10 business days to show it on your statement. The exact time depends on the bank, not on us.

## Shipping fees
The original shipping fee is refunded only when the item was defective, damaged or not what you ordered.

## Store credit
Instead of a refund to your card, you can choose store credit. Store credit is added to your account within 1 business day after inspection and does not expire.
