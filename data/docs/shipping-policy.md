# Shipping policy

## Where we ship
We ship to addresses in Turkey only. We do not ship internationally.

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for addresses in Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir.

## Dispatch cut-off
Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed later, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched on the next business day.

## Delivery guarantees
Delivery times are estimates from the carrier. We cannot guarantee delivery on a specific day or within a specific time slot, and the carrier does not offer appointment times.
