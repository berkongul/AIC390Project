# Warranty policy

## Warranty periods
Footwear is covered for 12 months against manufacturing defects. Outerwear and bags are covered for 24 months. All other clothing is covered for 6 months. The period starts on the delivery date.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as seams opening, zippers failing, soles separating and buckles breaking.

## What it does not cover
Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or accidents, and damage from washing against the care label are not covered.

## How to make a claim
Contact support with your order number and photos of the defect. The support team decides whether the item is repaired, replaced or refunded.
