# Wrong or missing items

## Wrong item received
If you received a different item from the one you ordered, report it within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item and its label. We email a free return label and ship the correct item as soon as the carrier scans the return.

## Missing items
If an item is missing from your parcel, report it within 7 days of delivery. We check the packing record and the parcel weight at our warehouse within 2 business days. Then we ship the missing item or refund it.

## Empty or opened parcels
If the outer packaging was opened or damaged when it arrived, take photos before you unpack and mention this in your report. This helps the carrier investigation.
