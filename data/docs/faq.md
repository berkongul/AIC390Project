# Frequently asked questions

## Can I return a sale item I opened?
Yes, unless it is labelled Final Sale, or it is socks or underwear. It must be unworn, unwashed and have its tags.

## How long does a refund take?
We issue the refund within 5 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Your bank may need up to 10 more business days to show it.

## Do you have a phone number?
No. Support works through the website chat and the help-center contact form.

## Can you guarantee a delivery time?
No. Delivery times are carrier estimates, and we cannot guarantee a specific day or time slot.

## When will an item be back in stock?
We do not announce restock dates. Use the Notify me button on the product page to get an email when it is back.
