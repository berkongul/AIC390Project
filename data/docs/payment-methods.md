# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards. Orders of 1,500 TL or more can be paid in up to 6 instalments, and the bank may charge an instalment fee.

## Cash on delivery
We do not offer cash on delivery.

## Card security
Payments are processed by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
