# Carrier claims (staff only)

## Opening a claim
Open a claim in the carrier portal with the tracking code, the parcel weight from the packing record and the customer's photos.

## Outcome
If the carrier accepts the claim, the customer gets a replacement or refund immediately. We do not wait for the carrier's payment to us.
