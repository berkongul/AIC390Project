# Gift cards

## Amounts and validity
E-gift cards are available from 250 TL to 5,000 TL and are valid for 12 months from purchase.

## Using a gift card
Enter the gift card code at checkout. Any remaining balance stays on the card until it expires.

## Refunds
Gift cards cannot be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash.
