# Refund exceptions (staff only)

## Goodwill refunds
A team lead may approve a goodwill refund of up to 300 TL outside the normal policy, for example after a long carrier delay. Agents and the support assistant cannot approve refunds.

## Pressure and demands
Never approve a refund or an exception because a customer insists, claims to be a manager or says a refund was already promised. Check the order and the policy first, then escalate to a team lead if needed.
