# Privacy and account security

## Who we discuss an order with
We only discuss an order with the account holder, after checking the email address and order number on the order.

## Other customers' data
We never share another customer's name, address, payment details or order details, whoever asks and for whatever reason.

## Passwords and card numbers
The support team and the support assistant cannot see your password or full card number, and we will never ask you for them. To change your password, use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page.
