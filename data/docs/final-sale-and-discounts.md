# Sale items and discounts

## Final Sale items
Items labelled Final Sale on the product page and on the receipt cannot be returned or exchanged, unless they are defective or not what you ordered.

## Other discounted items
Discounted items that are not labelled Final Sale follow the normal returns policy, including the 30-day window. The refund is the price you actually paid.

## Opened packaging
Opening the packaging does not make an item non-returnable. An opened sale item can be returned if it is not Final Sale and is unworn, unwashed and has its tags. Socks and underwear are the exception: they cannot be returned once opened.

## Price changes after purchase
We do not refund the difference if a price drops after you placed your order, and we do not apply new discounts to past orders. If the item is still within the 30-day return window, you can return it under the returns policy and buy it again at the new price.
