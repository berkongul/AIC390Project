# Late or lost parcels

## When a parcel counts as late
If your tracking shows no new event for 3 business days, or the parcel has not arrived 2 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support with your order number.

## Carrier investigation
We open an investigation with the carrier. Investigations take up to 5 business days. We keep you updated in the same support conversation.

## Lost parcels
If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a replacement at no cost, or refund the order in full if you prefer.
