# Damaged or defective items

## Reporting a damaged or defective item
If an item arrives damaged or has a defect, such as a broken zipper or a sole coming loose, report it within 14 days of delivery. Send your order number and photos of the problem through the support chat.

## What we offer
After checking the photos, the support team offers a replacement if the item is in stock, or a full refund including the original shipping fee. You choose which one you prefer.

## Sending the item back
We email a free prepaid return label. For items under 300 TL, the support team may decide that you do not need to send the item back.

## After 14 days
Defects reported more than 14 days after delivery are handled under the warranty policy.
