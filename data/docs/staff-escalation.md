# Escalation procedure (staff only)

## When to escalate
Escalate to a team lead when a customer reports a safety issue with a product, when a refund or goodwill gesture is requested outside the policy, when a carrier investigation has passed 5 business days, or when a customer has had no reply for more than 1 business day.

## How fast
Team leads review escalations within 4 working hours.
