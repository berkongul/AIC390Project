# Shipping fees

## Standard delivery
Standard delivery costs 59 TL. It is free for orders of 750 TL or more after discounts.

## Express delivery
Express delivery costs 99 TL for every order, including orders over 750 TL.

## Returns and exchanges
Return shipping is covered in the returns policy. Exchanges are free.
